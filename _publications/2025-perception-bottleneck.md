## Abstract

This paper investigates the perception bottleneck that limits vision-language models' performance in chart understanding tasks. We identify key limitations in visual perception and propose methods to improve chart understanding capabilities.

## Authors
Junteng Liu (First Author), Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, Junxian He
