Biography
======
I am a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group in Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Previously, I graduated with a B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was advised by Professor Junxian He and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.
