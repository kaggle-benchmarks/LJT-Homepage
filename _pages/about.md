Welcome to my academic homepage! I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. 

## Research Interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interests in:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Developing methods to improve logical reasoning capabilities in large language models
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**: Understanding and mitigating hallucination problems in vision-language systems
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Analyzing the internal mechanisms that govern truthfulness in language models
